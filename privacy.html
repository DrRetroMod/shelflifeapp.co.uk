Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Shelf Life is a personal app made and used by Dr. RetroMod. It runs on his own computer and connects only to his own accounts. This policy explains what it does with information from those accounts.

Who uses Shelf Life

Only Dr. RetroMod. Shelf Life isn't offered to the public, has no sign-up, and doesn't collect information about anyone else: not followers, not viewers, not visitors to this website.

What Shelf Life accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the connected channel. Shelf Life uses it only to upload Shorts that Dr. RetroMod has prepared and approved. It doesn't read, change or delete other videos, comments, subscribers, or any other Google account data.
  • Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok: permission to publish posts to Dr. RetroMod's own accounts (and, where needed, to read the account's name to confirm the connection). Nothing else is read.
  • Game information comes from public databases (IGDB, and iiDB for music clips). This involves no personal data.

Where information is kept

The sign-in tokens that let Shelf Life post, and the posts it prepares, are stored only on Dr. RetroMod's own computer. Shelf Life has no servers or database of its own on the internet. Images and videos for Instagram may be placed briefly in Dr. RetroMod's own cloud storage so Instagram can fetch them.

Sharing

Shelf Life doesn't sell, rent or share any information. Data from an account is only ever sent back to that same service, to publish a post that Dr. RetroMod chose to publish. It isn't used for advertising, and it isn't used to train AI models.

Google API services

Shelf Life's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in Shelf Life (Settings → Accounts → Disconnect), or from each service directly, for example at Google account permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored sign-in from the computer.

Changes

If Shelf Life starts doing anything new with account data, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.

Contact
