Video game birthdays, from a real collection

Shelf Life is a personal app made by Dr. RetroMod.

Shelf Life keeps track of when games, series and consoles first came out, and prepares posts celebrating their birthdays using photos from Dr. RetroMod's own collection. It also prepares other collection posts, such as spotlights, trivia and "where is this music from?" quizzes. Dr. RetroMod reviews the posts, and Shelf Life publishes them to his own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok).

Who can use it?
Shelf Life runs on Dr. RetroMod's own computer and posts only to his own accounts. It isn't a public service: there are no sign-ups, and no one else's account or data is used.

What it does with connected accounts

  • Uploads the posts and videos Dr. RetroMod has prepared and approved.
  • Nothing else: it doesn't read messages, contacts, followers or other private data.

Details are in the privacy policy.
